CITIZEN FIRST-AID PROTOCOL

Emergency Incident Response Checklist

1

Financial Fraud "Golden Hour" — Call 1930 Immediately

Within 2 hours of unauthorized debit or UPI transfer, call 1930 (Toll-Free). Law enforcement logs the complaint into the Citizen Financial Cyber Fraud Reporting System (CFCFRMS), automatically blocking the recipient account balance.

2

Lock Compromised Banking & Cards

Immediately use your bank’s official mobile app or emergency customer support to hotlist/block credit/debit cards, lock netbanking, and temporarily disable international UPI transactions.

3

Preserve All Digital Evidence (Do Not Delete)

Take high-resolution screenshots of transaction SMS (showing UTR/RRN numbers), WhatsApp or Telegram chat conversations, fraudulent phone numbers, email headers, website links, or malicious APK files received.

4

File Formal Complaint at cybercrime.gov.in

Lodge an online complaint on the official National Cyber Crime Reporting Portal. Note down the acknowledgement number. This acknowledgement is required by banks and police stations for refund settlement and FIR registration.

5

Women & Child Cyber Harassment Support

For cyber stalking, deepfakes, extortion, or objectionable content, reports can be submitted anonymously on cybercrime.gov.in or by calling Women Helpline 1090 / 181.
